04Questions

Before you register

56 answers about BitFest and each contest. Can't find yours? Write to bitfest@cse.kuet.ac.bd.

  • University students from across Bangladesh. Teams run from two members (3 Minute Thesis) to four (Soccer Bot and Capture The Flag); IUPC teams of three bring a coach, and gaming entries go from solo FIFA and Clash Royale players to Valorant teams of five.

  • The onsite finals are on Friday 5 and Saturday 6 February 2027 on the KUET campus in Khulna, and CSE Night closes the festival on the evening of the 6th. Online rounds run before that, from 13 to 22 January.

  • Registration opens on 30 November 2026 and closes on 12 January 2027. Fees are per team: BDT 1,000 to 2,000 for most contests and BDT 5,500 for IUPC. The AI Hackathon preliminary is free, with BDT 2,500 for each team selected for the final.

  • The AI Hackathon and the Datathon each run an online preliminary for 150+ teams; the top 60 and the top 30 come to KUET for the finals. The IT Business Case written round picks 20 teams. Finalists are announced on 24 January.

  • BDT 7,00,000 in cash prizes across the ten competitions. Each contest's share is announced with its rulebook.

  • The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at KUET, with four student clubs: SGIPC runs IUPC; KMiNDS the AI Hackathon, the Datathon and 3MT; HACK the Project Showcase, Line Following Robot and Soccer Bot; KCSC Capture The Flag. Write to bitfest@cse.kuet.ac.bd.
